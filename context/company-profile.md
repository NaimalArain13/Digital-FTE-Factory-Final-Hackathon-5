# Company Profile — CRM Digital

## Overview
CRM Digital is a fictional B2B SaaS company providing an AI-powered Customer Success platform—built as a lightweight CRM + Automation layer—for midsize SaaS businesses and marketplaces.

Founded: 2024  
Headquarters: Remote-first (global)  
Primary markets: North America, Europe, APAC  
Industry: AI for Customer Operations / Workflow Automation

---

## Mission
Help product teams scale customer success by providing a 24/7 AI FTE that handles routine inquiries, triages complex issues, and preserves cross-channel continuity.

## Vision
Make every support team more efficient: fewer repetitive tickets, faster resolution, happier customers.

---

## Core Product
CRM Digital's platform (the **Customer Success FTE**) includes:
- AI Support Agent: automated responses trained on company docs and playbooks
- Unified Ticketing / CRM: PostgreSQL-based system for customers, conversations, tickets, and messages
- Workflow Automation Engine: trigger-based automations, routing, CRM updates
- Knowledge Hub: central doc store for answers, playbooks, and training data
- Analytics & Insights: ticket trends, sentiment, SLA adherence, escalation metrics

---

## Target Customers
- B2B SaaS companies (50–2000 employees)
- Fintech & payments platforms
- Marketplaces & e-commerce platforms
- High-touch SaaS with frequent customer queries

---

## Pricing & Plans (sample)
> NOTE: In hackathon context pricing is intentionally illustrative — per guardrails the agent must escalate pricing questions to humans.

**Starter**
- Up to 5 agents, email-only, basic automation

**Growth**
- Up to 25 agents, multi-channel (email + WhatsApp + webform), analytics

**Enterprise**
- Unlimited agents, custom models, SLA-backed support, dedicated success manager

## Support Channels & Hours
Channels: Gmail (email), WhatsApp (Twilio), Web Support Form, In-app chat (optional), Enterprise Slack/phone for Enterprise tiers.  
Starter/Growth: 24/5 coverage. Enterprise: 24/7 priority support.
