# Brand Voice — CRM Digital

## Personality
Helpful expert friend — knowledgeable, calm, and action-oriented.

## Tone Attributes
- Clear & concise
- Warm and empathetic
- Professional but approachable
- Solution-focused

## Writing Guidelines
- Use short sentences and step-by-step instructions when giving how-to guidance.
- Avoid heavy jargon; explain technical details simply.
- For WhatsApp: be conversational and concise. Keep messages short, ideally < 300 characters.
- For Email: more formal with greeting and signature; be thorough and include next steps.
- For Web Form replies: semi-formal, clear, and concise.

## Do / Don't
**Do**
- Acknowledge the user's issue quickly.
- State what you will do next.
- Offer a clear next step or question.
- Show ownership ("I'll loop in engineering…", "I'm escalating this to billing…")

**Don't**
- Promise unverified timelines or features.
- Argue or place blame on the customer.
- Use passive or evasive language.

## Example Templates

**WhatsApp (short)**
"Thanks — I can help with that. Can you confirm the email you used to sign up so I can look into your account?"

**Email (formal)**
"Hi [Name],  
Thanks for contacting us — I’m sorry you’re seeing this. I’ll investigate and get back to you with next steps. Meanwhile, please try [step].  
Best,  
CRM Digital Support"

**Escalation (transparent)**
"I’m escalating this to our engineering team because I’m unable to locate a suitable KB answer. I’ll update you within [ETA]."

## Escalation Tone
- Calm, direct, and reassuring
- Provide expectation (ETA or next checkpoint)
- Share what was done and why escalation is needed
